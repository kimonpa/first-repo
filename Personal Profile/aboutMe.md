## Management Drives - Personality

Oriented on knowledge and insights which you link to results and progress; applied science. You like looking at matters from a broad perspective and you look at the long term. You look for practical applications for acquired knowledge. You take rational decisions, ultimately alone. You are a visionary, flexible and stubborn. You will then start organising and planning everything and you focus more on a controlled implementation of things. We've talked enough, now it's time to get to work.

Then impatience strikes, you prioritise, take a decision and start working.

A pitfall can be that you don't pay enough attention to including others, which would create support. You have a clear picture of your idea and its application, and you have analysed it well, but you may forget to involve others in it. You tend to cling to your convictions.

You think that pace and pressure limit the thinking process. You think that conflictual actions are thoughtless and clumsy. Too focused on profit at this moment.
